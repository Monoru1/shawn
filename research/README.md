# Research archive

This folder separates documented facts from public copy. A source can inform the
design without every fact appearing on the site.

Statuses:

- VERIFIED: confirmed by a primary source.
- CORROBORATED: confirmed by several coherent sources.
- PENDING: plausible, but still needs a primary confirmation.
- DO NOT PUBLISH: insufficiently documented or contradicted.

Private feedback and audio may guide design decisions. It must never be presented
as a public quote without Shawn's explicit permission.
